# Consent Management

## When to use
- Enforcing patient preferences on who can access or share their data.
- Recording consent scope, purpose, and expiration.

## Instructions
1. Record consent with patient identity, scope (data categories), purpose, and validity window.
2. Enforce consent checks on each access request; deny or filter data as required.
3. Log consent decisions and access attempts for auditability.
4. Provide clear responses indicating granted, denied, or partial access with reasons.
